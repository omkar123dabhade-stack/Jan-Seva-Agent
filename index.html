Government Schemes & Subsidies

Eligibility varies by scheme. Generally, EWS (Economically Weaker Sections) requires an annual family income below ₹8 Lakhs. Always check specific scheme guidelines like PM-KISAN (landholding farmers) or PM-JAY (SECC 2011 database).
You can apply online via dedicated portals (like pmkisan.gov.in) or offline at Common Service Centres (CSCs), Tehsil offices, or Gram Panchayat offices.

Taxes & GST

Visit the Income Tax e-Filing portal (incometax.gov.in). Go to the 'Link Aadhaar' section under Quick Links. Enter your PAN, Aadhaar number, and name as per Aadhaar to submit the request.
Under the new tax regime (default), income up to ₹3 Lakh is exempt. Slabs scale up gradually. Rebates under Section 87A often make income up to ₹7 Lakh tax-free. Consult the latest budget documents for exact percentages.

Pensions & Retirement

You can check your PF balance and request withdrawals using the EPFO Member e-Sewa portal or the UMANG mobile app by logging in with your UAN (Universal Account Number) and password.
Pensioners can submit Jeevan Pramaan using the Face RD app on smartphones, at local CSCs, bank branches, or via Postmen utilizing the India Post Payments Bank doorstep service.

Certificates & Documents

Applications can be made on your State's e-District portal online, or physically at the local Tehsildar / SDM office or a nearby Common Service Centre (CSC / Maha-e-Seva Kendra).
